# Check the dates in a KeepFinding import

Try a small export in a guest library before moving your bookmarks. Use a fresh signed-out browser session for each sample.

| Export | Test file | Saved date in the file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pocket | [Pocket CSV](pocket-test.csv) | 2025-11-01 UTC |
| Raindrop | [Raindrop CSV](raindrop-dates-test.csv) | 2024-03-01 UTC |

1. Open a sample CSV in GitHub. On desktop, choose **Download raw file**. On a phone, open **Edit and raw actions** and choose **Download**.
2. In a signed-out browser, open the matching [Pocket importer](https://keepfind.ing/pocket?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=mcasp-bookmark-date-check) or [Raindrop importer](https://keepfind.ing/raindrop?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=mcasp-bookmark-date-check).
3. Choose **Import your Pocket export** or **Import your Raindrop export** and select the sample. The preview should recognize the format and show one link.
4. Choose **Import 1 link** to save the sample. Close Settings and find the sample in **All saved**. Compare the card’s **Saved** age with the date in the table. These samples should show an age in years. Reload and confirm the sample and its **Saved** label are the same.

If the preview looks right, choose your own export from the same import panel and review the preview before saving it.
